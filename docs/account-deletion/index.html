XpenseTracker Account & Data Deletion

Submit an authenticated or email-based permanent deletion request without needing mobile app access.

What Gets Deleted Permanently

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, categories, transactions, budgets, recurring rules, lending agreements & repayments, consent logs, and Supabase authentication credentials. Local SMS queues and offline caches on your device are wiped when performed from the app (Settings → Privacy & Data Center → Delete Account).
